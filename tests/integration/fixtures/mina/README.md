# Mina's Brave Little Hello — local preview artwork

Five original fictional storybook illustrations generated with the built-in
image-generation tool on 2026-11-25, after Parham explicitly approved the set.
No photographs, external stock art, or private child portraits were used.
These images are sample content, not evidence of a live Dify/provider run.

The five 1024 × 1536 images are encoded as WebP for the local fixture:
`cover.webp`, `page-1.webp` through `page-4.webp`. Full-size PNG originals remain
in the generating Codex session's generated_images directory; fixture assets
are self-contained and do not depend on that local path.

Prompt set: original warm gouache and colored-pencil children's picture-book
art with soft paper texture, no lettering, watermark, panels or mockup frames.
Mina has brown skin, short dark curls, a teal pinafore, cream shirt and orange
backpack. Leo has sandy hair, round glasses and a mustard sweater. Pages use
the generated cover as a style/character reference. Scenes:

1. Cover: Mina and Leo at a welcoming school garden gate with paper birds.
2. Page 1: Mina arrives at the school gate, shy but curious.
3. Page 2: Mina helps Leo collect spilled pencils in the classroom.
4. Page 3: The children fold a paper bird together at an art table.
5. Page 4: The new friends run through the school garden with their birds.

Only the opt-in local demo uses this authored story. The default integration
fixture retains its original bytes and download-hash assertions.
